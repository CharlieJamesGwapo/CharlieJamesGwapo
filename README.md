## UI/UX & design systems

<picture>
  <source media="(max-width: 600px)" srcset="./assets/design-mobile.svg">
  <img src="./assets/design.svg" width="900" alt="Clear interfaces, consistent systems: tokens, accessible components, purposeful motion, and responsive layouts.">
</picture>

I approach design as part of engineering: how someone finds the next action, understands a state, recovers from an error, and moves through the product.

- **Reusable foundations** — color, typography, spacing, and motion tokens; consistent components and theme behavior.
- **Responsive experiences** — layouts that adapt to mobile and desktop, with readable content and usable controls.
- **Accessible interaction** — keyboard navigation, visible focus, descriptive labels, reduced-motion behavior, and understandable feedback.
- **Useful product states** — loading, empty, success, and error states that help people complete a task.

My portfolio demonstrates these ideas through expandable case studies, searchable projects, compact voice demos, and an optional interactive lab.
